Live: the map rewires itself

One stage, three windows, nothing else. Data Platform texts back "not us". The old line pulls back, the new one draws to Priya, then Tom lights up because Priya's out. The goal window only says what the current step is waiting on, in one iMessage bubble.

Zoom: departments, then people, then the conversation

Detail changes with zoom, not just size. Far out, each department is one orb and lines are bundled. Closer in, departments open into islands of people. All the way in, the people involved get names and roles and the live message appears next to them. Labels stay the same size on screen at every zoom.

Hook: one request, last quarter

The opening twenty seconds. A single request travels six departments while the day counter climbs to 9. Then the whole chain collapses into the one line it should have been.

Receipt: the payoff

One number, told honestly. The org dims to just Leo's final route. The hero says 3 hours against 4 days, and the week bar is drawn to true scale, so Leo's sliver is the point, not a bug.

Glass is the frame, never the page. Windows 7 kept every word on opaque white and put glass only on title bars and the taskbar. Microsoft's own guideline: glass in small regions, without text.

Built for the back row. Nothing essential under 24 px at 1920 wide, primary labels at 32, the hero at 180. Ink on white is 15.8:1, white on the stage is 11.6:1.

Color only where it has a job. Departments aren't colored at all, they're named and placed. The live route stands out by brightness and motion, so it never clashes with the line colors.

Moves feel like Windows 7, timed by research. Camera moves take about 1 second, then the line, then the label: one stage at a time. Pulses run at most twice. The full research report is in research/frutiger-research.md.
